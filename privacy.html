McReviewy Privacy Policy

McReviewy browser extension · Publisher: Shane Ison · Effective 7 November 2026

McReviewy is a PDF viewer for reviewing building drawings. It runs entirely in your browser. The publisher runs no server, collects no data, and receives nothing from the extension: no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reports and no advertising.

What the extension reads

  • PDF documents you open. McReviewy replaces the browser's PDF viewer, so when you open a PDF on any website or from your computer, the extension loads that file to show it. To do this it needs access to all sites and sees the address of PDFs you open. It does not read, record or send the other pages you visit.
  • City of Calgary permit pages. When you are signed in to the City of Calgary's DBA permit site (dbapermit.calgary.ca) and open a project page, the extension reads the project number, address, land-use district and project type shown in the page's header, so drawings for that project can be checked against the address. It may also fetch a project's summary and details pages using your existing sign-in. It never writes or submits anything to that site.
  • Your activity in the viewer, for the review timer. If the permit timer is on, the extension records how long a review is open and pauses when the computer is locked or idle. It uses the browser's idle signal only for this and does not record what you do.

What is stored, and where

Everything is kept in your browser's own extension storage on your device:

DataWhy
Markups, measurements and review notes for each drawingSo your work is still there when you reopen the drawing
Permit project records (project number, address, land-use district, project type, page address); the most recent 300 are keptTo check drawings against the project address
Review timer log (time spent per review)To show the time spent on each permit
Values entered in the RSI and H2K calculatorsSo the calculators remember your last inputs
Settings, keyboard shortcuts and scripts you addTo keep your preferences

Settings may be synced between your own devices by your browser's sync (for example, your Microsoft account in Edge) if you have browser sync turned on. That sync is provided by the browser, not by McReviewy. When you save markups into a PDF, they are written into the PDF file you save.

Network requests the extension makes

  • The PDFs you open, from the address you opened them from (including a second file you choose to compare).
  • dbapermit.calgary.ca: project pages, using your own sign-in, as described above.
  • data.calgary.ca: the City of Calgary's public building permit data, looked up by permit number to find a permit's address. Only the permit number is sent.
  • trustlist.adobe.com: Adobe's public list of trusted certificates, downloaded to check digital seals on drawings. Nothing about you or your documents is sent.

No other requests are made, and no data is sent to the publisher or any third party.

Sharing and sale

The publisher does not collect, sell, rent or share any personal information. No data is used for advertising, profiling or credit decisions.

Your control

  • The Options page can turn off the automatic PDF take-over, or limit it to chosen sites.
  • The Options page can export the extension's stored data to a file, or replace it with a file you choose.
  • Removing the extension deletes everything it stored in the browser.
